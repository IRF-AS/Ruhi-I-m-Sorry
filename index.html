A letter I struggled to write

For Ruhi 🖤

Kuch baatein hain jo shayad main saamne se nahi keh paata.

🖤

Dear Ruhi,

Please, isse aaram se padhna. Ye letter tumhe blame karne ke liye nahi, balki apne dil ki baat honestly kehne ke liye hai.

Ruhi,

I think tum sach mein mujhse better deserve karti ho, kyunki maine tumhe pehle se hi bahut hurt kiya hai. And idk, ab mujhe aisa feel ho raha hai jaise maine tumhe cheat kiya ho. Pata nahi kyun, bas ye guilt mujhe andar se kha raha hai.

Mujhe jab bhi emotional support ki zaroorat hoti hai, I feel like mere aas-paas koi nahi hota. Game mein jab tumne mere cage ki jagah 24 chhod kar 25/26 liya, tab mujhe samajh aa gaya tha ki kuch toh gadbad hai. Phir bhi main tumhare paas aaya.

Uske baad, jaise tum pehle khelti thi, mujhe burger deti thi aur meri ek life jaane ke baad bhi mera khayal rakhti thi, aaj mujhe waisa kuch feel nahi hua. Isiliye maine game beech mein hi chhod diya aur leave kar diya, bina exact reason bataye.

Idk, tumhe shayad ye bhi feel nahi hua hoga ki maine game kyun chhoda, kyunki mujhe lagta hai ki ye sab meri wajah se hi ho raha hai. It's called karma, right? Hmmm...

Maine abhi tak apne exams ke liye properly prepare bhi nahi kiya hai. Mere andar na motivation hai, na energy. Main already itna exhausted feel kar raha hoon ki samajh nahi aa raha sab kuch kaise handle karun. Kabhi-kabhi lagta hai ki main apni life mein sab kuch lose karta ja raha hoon.

Mujhe aisa feel hota hai ki koi meri baat dhang se nahi sunta, koi mere saath rukta nahi. Bhai ne bhi mujhe reality dikha di hai. Isiliye agar tum bhi mujhse gussa ho jao ya mujhe bura-bhala kehne lago, toh shayad mujhe surprise nahi hoga.

Idk maine aisi kaunsi galti kar di hai ki main khud ko itna blame kar raha hoon. Maybe maine bahut si cheezein galat ki hain, aur main unse inkaar nahi kar raha. But Ruhi, mujhe apni galtiyon ka ehsaas hai, aur mujhe sach mein afsos hai.

Please, mujhe har ek cheez ke liye maaf kar dena. Shayad main abhi woh better ladka nahi ban paaya jo tum deserve karti ho. Lekin shayad tum apne liye ek aisa ladka deserve karti ho jo tumhe khush rakhe, tumhari feelings samjhe aur tumhe woh care de jiski tum haqdar ho.

Mere paas ek hi achievement thi, and that was winning your heart. Aur ab mujhe darr lag raha hai ki kahin main woh bhi na kho doon. Idk mujhe apni journey dobara kahan se continue karni chahiye. I just want to restart my life, apni galtiyon ko samajhna chahta hoon aur dheere-dheere khud ko better banana chahta hoon.

Main khud ko us ghode jaisa feel karta hoon jo race mein ek bhi kadam aage badhaye bina hi haar gaya ho. Jaise shuruaat karne se pehle hi sab kuch khatam ho gaya ho. But shayad mujhe abhi khud ko sambhalna aur aage badhna seekhna hai.

Ruhi, I really apologise to you from the bottom of my heart. Main tumse ye expect nahi karta ki tum mujhe turant maaf kar do ya meri har baat samajh jao. Tumhari feelings bhi matter karti hain, aur tumhe jo feel hota hai, woh feel karne ka poora haq hai.

Main bas chahta hoon ki tumhe pata ho ki mujhe apni galtiyon ka ehsaas hai. I am genuinely sorry for every moment when I hurt you, disappointed you, or failed to make you feel loved and valued.

I genuinely hope tumhari life ab better ho, tumhe peace mile, tum khush raho aur tumhe woh sab mile jo tum deserve karti ho. Chahe aage humari journey jaisi bhi ho, main dil se chahta hoon ki tum theek raho.

Aur Ruhi, please ye mat sochna ki tumhari happiness meri galtiyon ka bojh uthana hai. Tumhe apne liye jo sahi lage, woh choose karne ka poora haq hai.

I'm truly sorry, Ruhi. 🖤

Dil se maafi chahta hoon. 🙏🏻

SOME WORDS FROM A TIRED HEART 🖤
MADE WITH HONESTY, NOT PERFECTION
